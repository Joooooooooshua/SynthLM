1. The Two Connected Halves of SynthLM

SynthLM bridges musical intuition with real-time audio code. It is structured into two cooperating halves:

  • The Instrument (Web Audio Graph): A polyphonic, dual-oscillator subtractive synthesizer with filter, ADSR envelope, 4-chain effects rack (Drive, Chorus, Delay, Reverb), and a 60fps real-time oscilloscope visualizer.
  • The AI Copilot (Two-Stage Synesthetic Reasoner): Powered by Google's flagship models (gemini-4-argon / gemini-3.8-flash) via @google/genai, translating poetic and emotional sound descriptions into validated acoustic parameters with instant Alpha / Beta A-B comparison.

3. The 3-Stop Code Tour

STOP 1 The Audio Signal Graph: src/audio/synthEngine.ts

Open synthEngine.ts. Notice lines 55–130 inside init(): each module is an instance of a Tone.js audio node connected via .chain() into a unified audio stream. Notice how applyPatch() uses .rampTo() for parameter updates to prevent audible clicks or pops when tweaking knobs or toggling A/B.

STOP 2 The Synesthetic Reasoner & Wave Sculptor: src/services/geminiSoundDesigner.ts & src/audio/waveMath.ts

Open geminiSoundDesigner.ts and waveMath.ts. Look at SYNTH_RESPONSE_JSON_SCHEMA and requestAISoundDesign(): for poetic metaphors like "像冰岛冬天极光一样的孤独感音色" or "玻璃泛音", the AI can switch oscillators to custom and sculpt 16 Fourier harmonic partials. In waveMath.ts, Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) seamlessly translates between single-cycle drawn curves and band-limited audio partials!

STOP 3 The Central State & Highlight Lifecycle: src/App.tsx

Open src/App.tsx. Look at handleSendMessage(): before applying the AI's new sound, it automatically copies the current state into alphaPatch, sets the new sound into betaPatch, and marks changed keys in highlightedParams. Look at handleClearHighlight(): the moment you click or drag a highlighted knob on the synth rack, only that specific knob's halo clears.

4. "To Change This, Start Here" Quick Pointers

  • Custom Demo Loop Melody: Edit src/audio/demoMelody.ts (or click the Music icon in the top bar to paste your melody notes in the UI).
  • Adding New Preset Sounds: Add entries to FOUNDATIONAL_PRESETS in src/audio/presets.ts.
  • Fine-tuning AI Prompts: Modify SYNTH_DESIGNER_SYSTEM_PROMPT in src/services/geminiSoundDesigner.ts.
  • Adjusting Visual Theme: Palette variables and pixel styles live in src/index.css.

5. A Practice to Reuse: Schema-Constrained Domain Modeling

When building an AI assistant for a complex domain with strict physical boundaries (like audio DSP, financial ledgers, or 3D graphics), never let the LLM generate unconstrained text or arbitrary numbers.

By defining a complete, validated TypeScript interface (SynthPatch), a strict JSON Schema (SYNTH_RESPONSE_JSON_SCHEMA) with the Gemini Interactions API, and a deterministic fallback clamper (clampPatch), you guarantee that the AI's creative intelligence produces safe, stable, zero-crash parameter updates every single turn.
